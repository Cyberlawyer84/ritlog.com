Version 2.2 — November 2026

RitLog 2.2 is here

The new rate of €0.25 per kilometre, an extra large widget, iPhone Duo support and a full backup. See what's new in one minute.

The video is in Dutch.

Important
2026 mileage rate: €0.25 per km
From 1 January 2026 the tax-free mileage allowance in the Netherlands is €0.25 per kilometre. RitLog suggests setting this rate for each vehicle. Trips up to and including 2025 stay at €0.23: the app uses the rate that applied on each trip's date, in your exports and in the yearly amount on the Home screen too.
New
Extra large widget
On iOS 27 you can now add RitLog as an extra large widget to your Home Screen: your odometer, the split between business, commuting and private, and your latest trips. All at a glance, without opening the app.
New
Made for iPhone Duo
Unfold your iPhone Duo and RitLog uses the full large screen. Home shows two columns, your trips appear with their details alongside, and Insights is neatly split around the fold.
New
Full backup and restore
Alongside the quarterly CSV files, RitLog now keeps a complete copy of all your data in a folder you choose, for example in iCloud Drive. Use it to restore everything on a new iPhone. And a restore can be undone too.
New
Export expenses and move trips
Export your fill-ups, charging sessions and other costs as CSV for your accountant. Logged a trip on the wrong vehicle? Move it to another vehicle of the same type.
Improved
More reliable and easier to read
Arrival and departure reminders are more reliable, and RitLog warns you when "Precise Location" is off. When you enter a trip, the app checks the odometer for the chosen date. PDF exports follow the app language, the app scales with larger text and works better with VoiceOver. Plus many small improvements.
Version 2.0 — June 2026

RitLog is completely redesigned

A new look, smarter logging and handy widgets. These are the highlights of the biggest update RitLog has ever had.

Design
Warm, fresh new look
The entire interface has been given a completely new coat. Goodbye cold blue — RitLog now uses a warm cream-green palette that feels nicer for everyday use. Every card, every screen, every detail has been rethought. On top of that, the app automatically adapts to your phone's setting: choose light, dark, or let your phone decide for you.
New
Widget on your home screen and lock screen
RitLog now has widgets for your home screen — in small, medium and large sizes — plus a widget for your lock screen. At a glance you see your current mileage and the amount still to be claimed. And with a single tap you log a new trip straight away, without opening the app. Just like the app, the widget automatically adapts to light or dark.
Unique feature
For the bike too
RitLog now also supports business bike trips — something you rarely come across in other trip logging apps. For bike trips you log the distance in kilometres (after all, a bike has no odometer), so you can also account for your cycling kilometres correctly for the tax authorities. Simply through the same familiar screen as your car trips.
Improved
Multiple vehicles
Do you have a company car and a private car (or a bike)? You can now create multiple vehicles and keep track of your trips and mileage per vehicle. Switching between vehicles takes just one tap from the home screen. All export overviews are neatly split per vehicle.
Improved
Smarter locations and addresses
When logging a trip, the app now automatically suggests addresses based on your input — thanks to integration with Apple Maps. Locations you visit more often are recognised and automatically linked to your trips. That way you only have to enter the address of your office or client once.
Improved
Enter your starting mileage right away
New the first time you launch: the app asks for your current mileage. That way your records are accurate from the very first trip — no more separate starting figure you have to correct manually later. If you don't fill in the reading, the app gives a clear warning so you don't forget anything.
Improved
Better PDF export
The PDF export now shows all columns correctly and in full — even longer categories like "Commuting" are no longer cut off. The vehicle's licence plate is listed separately per export. That way, at the end of the year you hand over an overview that's ready for your accountant right away.
New
New app icon
RitLog has a new, fresh app icon that matches the refreshed colour palette: a bright brand-green background with a clear route symbol. The icon stands out on your home screen and fits the app's modern look.
