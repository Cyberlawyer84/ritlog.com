RitLog Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Introduction

RitLog is an iOS app for trip logging and vehicle cost management. We care deeply about the privacy of our users. This privacy policy describes what data the app processes and how it is handled.

What data does RitLog process?

RitLog processes the following data that you enter yourself:

  • Trip data (date, times, departure and arrival location, odometer reading or distance, category, label)
  • Vehicle data (name, type, licence plate, odometer reading, rate)
  • Fuel, charging and other vehicle costs
  • Saved locations (name, address, coordinates)

Location data

With your permission, RitLog can use location services on your device for:

  • Reminders when you arrive at or leave your saved locations (geofencing), even when the app is closed
  • Location suggestions when logging a trip

Your location is determined on your device. RitLog does not keep a continuous location history and does not send your location to RitLog.

Maps, addresses and distances (Apple Maps)

To look up addresses, calculate routes and distances, and show maps, RitLog uses Apple's map services (Apple Maps). To do so, the addresses or coordinates involved are sent to Apple. Apple processes this data under Apple's privacy policy. RitLog itself receives none of it.

Motion data

With your permission, RitLog can use motion sensors (CoreMotion) to determine whether you are travelling by car, bike or on foot. This is used solely to avoid unnecessary reminders when you are not driving. This data is not stored or shared.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your iPhone itself. No RitLog server is involved.

Where is your data stored?

Your data is stored on your device. RitLog has no servers of its own, requires no account and does not receive your data. The widget shows a brief summary (such as your odometer, amounts and latest trips), which the app prepares for it on the same device.

Backup and export

If you turn on automatic backup, RitLog keeps a complete copy of your data and CSV files of your trips in a folder you choose, for example in iCloud Drive or with another storage service in the Files app. You can also export your data as a CSV or PDF file and share it yourself, for example by email. Wherever you choose to store it, the terms and privacy policy of that provider apply. RitLog has no access to or control over the location where you keep your backups and exports.

Third-party services

RitLog does not use:

  • Analytics services or tracking
  • Advertising networks
  • External login services
  • RitLog servers or cloud services

The only external services that data is exchanged with are Apple Maps (see above) and the storage service you choose yourself for your backup or export.

Importing contacts

RitLog lets you import addresses from your contacts as locations. This happens only locally on your device. Only the name and address you choose are saved as a location in RitLog. Your contacts are not shared with third parties.

Your rights

Because your data is on your own device, you are in full control:

  • You can view all your data within the app
  • You can edit or delete data
  • Deleting the app permanently deletes all data on your device
  • Backups and exports in the folder you chose remain there until you delete them yourself

Children

RitLog is not aimed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated. Changes are published on this page with an updated date.
